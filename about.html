OUR STORY

Built around the table.

Taste Yard started with a simple belief: some of life's best moments happen around food.

Not fancy food for the sake of being fancy. Just honest Nigerian flavours, proper portions, smoky grills and the kind of meals that make everybody at the table reach for another bite.

We call it the Yard because that's the feeling we're chasing — relaxed, lively and familiar.
